# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Traceboard is maintained as a disciplined design-systems engineering utility. Contributions must support clear technical reasoning, accessible product behavior, maintainable architecture, and respectful collaboration.

## Expected behavior

Contributors are expected to:

- Communicate precisely and respectfully.
- Critique implementation decisions rather than individuals.
- Provide reproducible evidence for reported defects.
- Respect accessibility, privacy, security, and licensing requirements.
- Keep pull requests focused, reviewable, and technically justified.
- Disclose conflicts of interest or third-party constraints when relevant.

## Unacceptable behavior

The following are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Deliberate disclosure of private, confidential, or security-sensitive information.
- Malicious code, credential submission, data exfiltration, or unauthorized access attempts.
- Misrepresentation of test results, review status, or implementation provenance.
- Repeatedly ignoring documented project decisions without presenting new evidence.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, reject, close, or remove contributions that violate this code. They may also restrict participation when necessary to protect the project or its contributors.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository administrator through the GitHub contact mechanism associated with this repository. Do not create a public issue containing sensitive personal or security information.

Reports will be reviewed as promptly and confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a person who makes a good-faith report is not acceptable.
